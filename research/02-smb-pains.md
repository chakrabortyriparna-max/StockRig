Quotes:
- Intuit community forum (⚠ search-verified; direct fetch transport error): "We're a small business. Been using QB for 20 years... 2023 it went up on subscription to $530. 2024 it went up to $689. 2025 it's going up to $999... "Robbery in daylight." — https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/other-questions/price-increase-2025/00/1546802
- Second Intuit community quote (⚠): "I used to pay $589... now I'm being asked to pay nearly $1,000 per year—a 70% increase—for the same basic features... Spread (✓ fetched): business2community.com — every QBO tier rose 15–25% May 1, 2026 (Plus: $90→$110/mo); Desktop licenses rose again Feb 2026 ($999→$1,149); ACH fees doubled ($3→$5); payroll ~+20%; est. ~3M businesses on Plus alone; cumulative Plus +64% since 2020. Second Aug 2026 wave reported by beancount.io pushing Plus toward $115–$275 tiers.

Inadequacy: switching costs are the bottleneck ("data export, conversion, training, accountant workflow"); users report one-way doors ("If you have recurring charges, ALL your data will be lost moving").

WTP: businesses already pay $1,300–$3,000+/yr and are actively shopping.

## #3 — Multi-Channel Ecommerce Payout Reconciliation

Quotes: Shopify Community threads (⚠): "keeping everything in sync becomes a full-time job... reconciling sales in accounting at month-end turns into a guessing game."
✓ fetched EcomCPA: "Ask an ecommerce bookkeeper what causes the most errors... payouts... If you book the $14,000 deposit as revenue... your books are wrong in at least nine ways simultaneously." — https://ecomcpa.com/the-payout-reconciliation-gap-why-amazon-shopify-and-tiktok-shop-deposits-are-wrecking-ecommerce-books-in-2026/

Inadequacy: A2X/Link My Books work but "$50-300 per month per channel," output still needs human review; all-in-one platforms pricier with lock-in. Sub-$1M sellers fall below tooling sweet spot → spreadsheets. Fee leakage $18K–$54K/yr at $3M GMV unrecovered.

WTP: proven ($600–$3,600/yr spent today); gap = affordability + cross-channel orchestration for sub-$1M sellers.
