## Bottom line
1. Premise survives: HCP & Jobber (where micro shops live) lack native van-stock tracking.
2. NOT first: Ply integrated into both; $15–45/mo scanner tier exists.
3. Differentiation MUST be: job-linked billing loop + tech-level par/restock at micro-shop pricing.
