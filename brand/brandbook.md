# StockRig Brand Book v1.0 — 2026-08-22

## Name
**StockRig** (noun: "rig" = a tradesperson's work van). Says what it does; survives being said out loud in a supply-house line.
Domain status (checked 2026-08-22, DNS only, nothing purchased): stockrig.com TAKEN · **stockrig.io AVAILABLE (recommended primary)** · getstockrig.com AVAILABLE (defensive) · stockbed/partrig/restockrig .com also available.

## Taglines
- Primary: **Know what's on the truck.**
- Billing-loop variant: Stop buying parts you already own.
- Restock variant: Your vans restock themselves. (aspirational — label as marketing)

## Positioning line
Van stock that closes the loop — par levels to billable parts for 1–5 tech shops.

## Logo
Concept: **The Par Line.** A cargo bed of bins against a dashed par-level line; the orange bin dips below it — the moment StockRig exists to catch.
Files: `logo-mark.svg` (badge), `logo-horizontal.svg` (lockup + tagline), mono variant = mark with #1C1E22/#F7F5F2 only.
Clear space: ½ bin-width on all sides. Minimum size: 32px badge.

## Color
| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary action / alert-of-record | Rig Orange | `#F25C05` | CTAs, low-stock state, logo accent |
| Ink | Charcoal | `#1C1E22` | Text, badge, dark UI |
| Support | Steel | `#8A9199` | Secondary text, inactive |
| Surface | Bed White | `#F7F5F2` | Backgrounds |
| Positive | Stock Green | `#2E9E6B` | In-stock, success |
| Danger | Alert Red | `#D64545` | Out-of-stock, destructive |

Rationale: hi-vis safety orange reads "jobsite," not "SaaS pastel"; charcoal+bed-white keeps it tool-like. Orange is reserved for things that need a tech's eyes NOW — never decorative.

## Typography
- Display/UI: Inter or system-ui stack (`Inter, 'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif`) — free, renders everywhere.
- Data/part numbers: monospace (`'JetBrains Mono', Consolas, monospace`) — part numbers must be scannable and unambiguous (no O/0 confusion in tables).
- Headings: 800–900 weight, tight tracking (-2%). Body: 400–500.

## Voice
Jobsite plain talk. Short sentences. No "streamline your workflow" slop. Numbers over adjectives. Never shame the tech — the van chaos is the industry's fault, not theirs.
Examples:
- ✅ "You own 14 thermostats. You can find zero."
- ❌ "Optimize your mobile inventory management journey."

## Motion
UI transitions ≤150ms, ease-out. The par-line dash is the only brand animation motif (marches leftward when a restock list generates).
